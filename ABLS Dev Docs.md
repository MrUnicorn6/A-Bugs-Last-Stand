# Towers

## Bettle

 The beetle uses close-range attacks to do decent damage to in-range bots with okay pierce.  
   
Upgrades-

1. Base  
2. Bigger ball- extra damage and pierce  
3. Stronger neck- increase in firing speed

   
3a- Scarab- gains a flash bomb when the ball runs out, stunning nearby droids  
4a auric gleam- increases range of stun and duration  
5a Iridescent Napalm- Adds burn to the flash and increased damage all-round  
   
3b-dung beetle- rolls semi-auto boulders down the path, sometimes pushing droids back.  
4b inferno ball- increase in speed and duration of ball  
5b Igneous Orb- Lights the path behind it in a foul smelling flame and explodes on death  
   
3c \-Atlas- Gains a Pinch Attack that grabs smaller droids and kills them over time  
4c prince of stags- faster destruction time  
5c King of Beetles-can grab smaller elites with a quicker cooldown on smaller enemies.

Upgrades-

1. Base

3. Better carapace-  improves the body, giving a buff to fire rate and access to cloak detection

3a-Fire Ant-adds fire damage to its bullet dealing extra damage over time  
4a-Burning Core- Increase to fire duration and damage  
5aLiving Furnace- turns projectiles into a stream of fire that burns everything in it’s sights

3b-Bullet Ant- Gains a major buff to its fire rate\!  
4bTermantator- Increase in pierce, adds a select target option to tower  
5b-Ant-I Machine-Mortar- Gains a slow firing missile that wastes away anything in its radius

3c Honeypot Ant- Gives an increase to firing rate to nearby towers   
4c Gracious Leader- Give nearby towers extra money gain on hit; increase in range  
5c-Royal Cauldron-Gains ability to double firing rate of all nearby towers for a limited time
